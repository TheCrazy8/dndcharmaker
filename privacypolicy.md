# Privacy Policy

## Effective Date

April 28, 2026

## Overview

This application ("FollyVTT") is a personal character management tool designed to help users create, edit, save, and manage tabletop RPG characters and optional source content.

Your privacy is important. This application is designed to minimize data collection and give users direct control over their own information.

---

## Information We Collect

The application may store the following information:

* Character sheet data created by the user
* Uploaded optional source files (such as spells, feats, subclasses, and monsters)
* Character portraits uploaded by the user
* Google Drive authentication tokens required for Drive saving/loading features

This information is only used to provide the app’s functionality.

---

## How Data Is Stored

### Local Storage

By default, character data and settings may be stored locally in your browser using localStorage.

This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to external servers by the application itself.

---

### Google Drive Integration

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the application may request permission to:

* Save character files to your Google Drive
* Load character files from your Google Drive
* Save and manage optional source files

The application uses Google OAuth authentication and only accesses files necessary for these features.

The app uses the following Google Drive permissions:

* `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file`
* `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata`

The application does not access unrelated files in your Google Drive.

---

## Data Sharing

This application does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

No analytics, advertising, or tracking services are used.

---

## Data Security

Reasonable efforts are made to protect your data; however, no method of electronic storage is completely secure.

Users are responsible for safeguarding their own Google account access and local device security.

---

## Children’s Privacy

This application is not specifically directed toward children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
